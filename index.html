How to read the geometry

The surface gives U(x, y). The horizontal plane fixes utility at ū. Their intersection projects to an indifference curve in 2D.

The budget plane rises vertically from the budget line. The orange curve on it gives utility along that line. Its highest point is the optimum.

For a smooth interior solution, the optimal indifference curve is tangent to the budget line: MRS = px/py. Corners and kinks have different optimality conditions.

Goods are nonnegative; all included preferences are increasing. The optimum exhausts the budget. Utility units and vertical scaling do not affect the optimal bundle.
